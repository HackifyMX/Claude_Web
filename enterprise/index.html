    LAYER 04 — COORDINATION

    The system that makes it all operate as one

    Individual agents solve individual problems. An orchestrated agent network solves entire business processes — simultaneously, without collision. This is the coordination layer that makes an AI Powered Enterprise actually work.

    • Real-time, multi-agent coordinationAcross sales, support, finance, and operations.
    • Distributed execution, centralized directionEach agent acts. The orchestrator directs.
    • Modular by designNew agents integrate without disrupting existing operations.
    • Human checkpoints where they matterCritical decisions remain under human authority.
